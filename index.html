Support

Need help with Passeo? Here are the answers to common questions. Can’t find yours? Email us at wennekes.am@gmail.com and we’ll get back to you.

How do I play?

Lay all the tiles on the pitch so the dotted route takes the ball from your player, past your teammates, into the goal next to the keeper. Tap a tile to turn it, drag it to place it. Level 1 shows you step by step, and you can watch that again in Settings.

I bought the full game, but it is locked

Open Settings in the app and tap “Restore purchase”. Use the same Apple account you bought it with.

Can my family play too?

Yes. The full game supports Family Sharing.

How do I keep my progress on a new iPhone?

Turn on iCloud for your Apple account. Passeo keeps your progress there and picks it up on your new iPhone.

I am stuck on a level

Tap the light bulb: a hint shows where one tile goes, see-through and turned the right way. You get three per level. Every level has exactly one solution.

How do I change the language or team colour?

Tap the gear on the career map to open Settings.
