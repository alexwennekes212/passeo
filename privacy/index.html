Privacy policy

In short

Passeo does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no tracking and no analytics.

Your progress

Your stars, records and settings are stored on your iPhone. If iCloud is turned on, they are also kept in your own iCloud account so you keep them on a new phone. Apple handles this; we cannot see it.

Game Center

If you use Game Center, your daily puzzle times and achievements go to Apple’s Game Center and are covered by Apple’s privacy policy. We do not receive any personal data from it.

Purchases

The full game is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details.

Crash reports

Diagnostic reports stay on your iPhone. You can choose to share them from the settings in the app. If you allow it in your iPhone settings, Apple may also share anonymous crash reports with us.

Children

Passeo is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
